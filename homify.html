Homify

Swap every homophone for a sound-alike: “I see the sea” → “Eye sea the see”

Input

Output

Use / avoid / keep

Comma-separated. Or tap words below to cycle them through use → avoid → keep → normal.

use avoid keep
Homophone groups

One group per line, words separated by commas. Each word may be in only one group. Words swap with the next word in their group (wrapping around), or a random one.

No-suffix words

Words that never take -s / -ed / -ing, or whose suffixed form no longer sounds like the rest of the group (“is” isn’t “eyes”). They’re never used as a suffixed word’s base or produced as a suffixed replacement. Separate with commas, spaces or new lines.
